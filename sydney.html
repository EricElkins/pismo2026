A Packing List for Pismo Beach

Three Nights  ·  August 20th through 23rd, 2026  ·  By Muggle Reckoning

Dear Miss Cohen,

You've been officially summoned to Pismo Beach for the Great 80th Birthday Celebration of Bubi and Tante Bubi. This is a matter of family, seawater, and cake, in roughly that order.

Below is your complete packing list. Everything is required. Unless it isn't.

The Wardrobe

Pismo mornings are cold and foggy; afternoons warm up. Pack for both.
  • "RuthLeslie 80" custom UPF sun shirt. Waiting for you at the reunion house. Wear it on the beach — it's your primary sun defense and a wearable Bubi + Tante Bubi souvenir all in one.
  • Two swimsuits. One dry, one drying. Rotate. (The UPF sun shirt above doubles as your cover-up — no separate one needed.)
  • A warm hoodie or sweatshirt. Do not skip this. Pismo mornings are shockingly cold and no one will believe you.
  • A windbreaker or light jacket for cliff walks.
  • Two pairs of long pants — jeans, leggings, whatever.
  • One pair of shorts for the warm afternoons.
  • Four or five t-shirts.
  • Pajamas.
  • Underwear × 5, socks × 5.
  • Sneakers for walking, plus flip-flops or sandals for the beach.

Slightly Dressier Attire

It's a pretty casual weekend, so a single "slightly nicer than beach clothes" outfit does it.
  • One slightly-dressier outfit — something that says "I love you, Bubi and Tante Bubi" without needing to say it out loud. Not beach clothes; not a ballgown either.
  • Optional but excellent: a handwritten card for each of them. Airplane time is the best time to make one — two pieces of blank paper, some good pens, and 40,000 feet of altitude will do most of the work for you.

The Beach Kit

  • Sun hat — wide brim, wizardly, or a baseball cap. Both acceptable.
  • Sunglasses. Muggle version. No anti-Basilisk enchantment required.
  • Reusable water bottle.
  • A beach towel (check with your mum first — the reunion house may provide).
  • Sunscreen — don't pack it. Remind your mum to put a bottle in the car when she leaves for the road trip. She's the SPF Ministry now. One less thing for you to fit in a 3-oz bottle.

A Note on Muggle Liquid Restrictions

Important: The Transportation Security Administration — a strict Muggle regulatory body — permits no liquid, gel, cream, or paste container larger than 3 ounces (100 mL) in your carry-on. This is a very odd law, but very firmly enforced, and no wand-waving is permitted at the security checkpoint.

Because you're flying with your dad, ask him nicely to put any full-size things in his checked bag. Otherwise, hunt down the little 3-oz travel versions of:

  • Shampoo, conditioner, body wash
  • Any skin-care lotions or creams
  • Toothpaste (or send a full-size in checked)

Sunscreen is handled by Mom on the road-trip side — see the Beach Kit above.

Everything else — toothbrush, hair ties, hair brush, chapstick, deodorant stick — goes in your regular bag with no restrictions. Solids fly free.

Required Reading

You are an avid reader. Pismo mornings are foggy and quiet. This is the greatest possible combination.
  • At least TWO books — three nights means three bedtimes, plus airplane time, and you know how you are.
  • A book light. Grown-ups sharing rooms will silently thank you.
  • A journal for anything worth remembering.
  • A pen. Or three. Pens have opinions and disappear.
A suggestion: If you're revisiting Hogwarts, Prisoner of Azkaban is the correct choice for coastal reading — dementors love the fog, and you'll get the reference. If you're reading something new instead, come find your Uncle Eric on day one and tell him what.

Objects of Interest

  • A deck of cards.
  • Uno, or a card game that plays with cousins.
  • Anything you'd like to teach the older cousins. They do not know as much as they think they do, and you are perfectly positioned to help with this.
  • A small bag for beach things (so towels don't drown your book).

A Cousin's Duty

Being the youngest of the cousins is an office, not a demotion.
  1. You are officially permitted to correct any adult who gets a Harry Potter fact wrong. Do so kindly, but firmly.
  2. You are the designated arbiter of who at the reunion is fun and who is boring. Your verdicts are final.
  3. You get first choice of seat at the birthday dinner. Uncle Eric will personally enforce this.
  4. You have been casting a wordless spell on Bubi and Tante Bubi since the day you were born. It has no incantation. It works by walking into the room. Bring the spell. The rest of this list is negotiable.

With considerable affection, and by the authority vested in me by no one in particular,

Uncle Eric
